    Install in two minutes

    REF CLIPS is in early access: the first time, your computer may show a security message. Here is what to do.

    1. Download REF CLIPS.dmg and open it.
    2. Drag the REF CLIPS icon into Applications.
    3. Open REF CLIPS. If the Mac refuses: System Settings › Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway. Only once.

    Questions

    Is it free?

    Yes, during early access, for every referee with a Referee Tracker account.

    Are my videos uploaded?

    No. Your videos, clips, notes and reports stay on your computer. If you agree, REF CLIPS only sends usage statistics to improve. Details in the privacy policy (in French).

    Which videos work?

    MP4, MOV, MTS, AVI and other camera and phone formats, up to 4K, in one or several files. Google Drive, Dropbox, OneDrive, WeTransfer and direct links too. YouTube does not allow downloads: ask the club for the file.

    Do I need internet?

    Only to sign in and to fetch a video from a link. Analysis and export work offline.

    We are a 3-person crew.

    Choose “3 referees” in the settings: the Centre calls and triple whistle appear.

    A problem or an idea?

    In REF CLIPS, open ⋯ › Report a problem: your message goes straight to the team.
